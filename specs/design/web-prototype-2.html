Sibillo design language

A house, not a dashboard

Sibillo lives in a home. The interface is warm paper and ink, quiet until something needs attention, with one red: the sprite's hat. Everything on every screen is built from the values on this page, and from nothing else.

Red is rare

The accent marks the one thing to do next (send, focus) and the sprite. Buttons are ink; switches are ink. A screen with five red things has none.

One of each

One button, one field, one select, one dialog. Two controls that do the same job look the same, on every screen.

Rows, not boxes

Settings are rows in a card: what it is on the left, the control on the right. Lists are rows too. Cards never nest.

Serif speaks, sans works

Newsreader is the voice of the house: greetings, page titles, quotes. Hanken Grotesk is everything you operate.

Nothing is ever blank

Every list has an empty state that says what it is for and offers the first action. Every wait shows that it is waiting.

Destructive is explicit

Danger shares the red family, so it never relies on color: it has its own verb, an icon, and a confirmation.

Color

Sixteen roles, each with a light and a dark value. Text on its background meets WCAG AA.

Type

Six sizes, three weights (400, 500, 600). No other size exists.

Elevation and motion

Three levels: on the page (a line), floating (menus, the message box), above the page (dialogs). Two durations, one curve; nothing moves when the device asks for reduced motion.

Components

Screens are assembled from these. A screen never styles a button, a field or a dialog of its own.

Button

Primary once per view, at most. Secondary for everything else; ghost inside toolbars; danger only for what cannot be undone.

Fields

Label above, hint or error below. A select looks like a field on every browser; the list it opens is the device's own.

Nothing is wrong with the dough: it is cold. At 18 °C bulk fermentation takes 10 to 12 hours, not eight.

Two ways to fix it

  • Give it time: wait until the dough has grown by about half and wobbles when you shake the bowl.
  • Give it warmth: the oven with only its light on holds about 26 °C.
